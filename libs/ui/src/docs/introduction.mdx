## Who is this for

<Unstyled>
  <Story of={Figures.Audiences} />
</Unstyled>

Every component page opens with two badges: its **status** — Core, Candidate, App-specific or Deprecated — and the **team that owns it**. Core is safe everywhere; anything else belongs to an application team until the core team promotes it. Definitions: [Contribute → Status and owner](?path=/docs/get-started-contribute--docs#status-and-owner).

## Maintainers

Routes for people who change the system rather than consume it. Nothing here is needed to build a screen.

- [Contribute](?path=/docs/get-started-contribute--docs) — propose, get the core team's decision, scaffold with `pds:component`, ship through the CI gates.
- [CSS architecture](?path=/docs/docs-css-architecture--docs) — where styles live (ITCSS layers, BEMIT names, `--p-*` bridges).
- [Token pipeline](?path=/docs/docs-token-pipeline--docs) — where values come from (Figma → `tokens.json` → generated `--pds-*`).
