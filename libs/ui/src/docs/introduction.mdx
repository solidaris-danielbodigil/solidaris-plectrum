## Who is this for

<Unstyled>
  <Story of={Figures.Audiences} />
</Unstyled>

Every component page opens with two badges: its **status** — Core, Candidate, App-specific or Deprecated — and the **team that owns it**. Core is safe everywhere; anything else belongs to an application team until the core team promotes it. Definitions: [Contribute → Status and owner](?path=/docs/get-started-contribute--docs#status-and-owner).
